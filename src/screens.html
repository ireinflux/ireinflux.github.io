EAGLE

APOLLO 11 · FINAL DESCENT · SEA OF TRANQUILITY · 20 JULY 1969

Ground elapsed time 102:42. You're Neil Armstrong, about 2,100 ft above the Moon. The computer is in P64 and is flying Eagle toward the planned landing site, which turns out to be a boulder field on the rim of West crater.

Take manual control (P66) by touching any control. Fly long, find a clear patch and set her down gently before the descent stage runs dry. Watch for the LOW LEVEL light and the "60 seconds" / "30 seconds" calls.
